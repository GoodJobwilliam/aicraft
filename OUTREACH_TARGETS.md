Contact only through public project channels (issues, discussions, or a maintainer's clearly published contact), follow each community's rules, and never send unsolicited bulk messages.

Use `OUTREACH_LOG.md` to record an actual contact or reply. Do not infer interest from stars, downloads, or repository similarity.

## Contact sequence

Start with a technical question tied to the public project. Share the free server only after a relevant reply or when the project's contribution policy permits a resource link. Offer the $49 Team Rules Pack only after a maintainer describes a concrete shared-rules or CI pain. Discuss Team Updates only with teams that ask for ongoing maintenance or rollout help.
