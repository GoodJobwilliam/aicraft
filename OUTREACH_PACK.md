## Direct message

I am looking for 3 small engineering teams to test a local-first code-review workflow. Could I ask how you currently handle review rules, CI gates, and false positives?

If the workflow is useful, I can include your language mix and rollout needs in the first Team Updates release.

## Qualification questions

1. How many engineers are on the team?
2. Which languages are reviewed most?
3. What is the current review path: editor, pull request, CI, or a mix?
4. What would make a monthly rules/support service worth paying for?

## Success criteria

- 20 targeted messages sent
- 5 substantive replies
- 3 teams willing to test
- 1 paid or explicitly pre-committed Team Updates customer

Do not count impressions, likes, or directory listings as customer validation.

For public replies, use the GitHub team-trial form so team size, workflow, timing, and offer signal are recorded consistently in the issue thread. Submitting the form creates no charge or subscription.
