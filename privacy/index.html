Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

1. What We Collect

We collect only the information you provide directly: email address (for account creation and license delivery) and payment information (processed securely by our payment provider — we never store credit card numbers).

When you purchase a product, we record your name, email, and the products purchased to provide license keys and support.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To deliver digital products and license keys
  • To provide customer support
  • To send product updates (only with your consent)
  • To improve our products based on aggregate usage data

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with:

  • Payment processors (Stripe, PayPal, Creem) — for transaction processing
  • Email services — for license delivery and support communication

These third parties are contractually bound to protect your data.

4. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you services. You may request deletion at any time by contacting us.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact us at the email below.

6. Contact
